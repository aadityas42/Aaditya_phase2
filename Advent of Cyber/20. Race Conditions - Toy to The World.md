# 20. Race Conditions - Toy to The World

## Solution:

- I lea
- We use BurpSuite for this challenge.
- We use the proxy and repeater options to exploit the race condition
- After setting up our environment, we go to the TBFC shop, log in using the given credentials and attempt to buy a limited edition SleighToy, of which 10 are there in stock
- After making a checkout request, we go to the proxt-->HTTP history-->process_checkout and send the exact request to the repeater
- Next, we go to the repeater and create a new tab group, then we duplicate this tab, then we click on parallel to launch 15 simultaneous requests to the server.
- This still sends the requests and we make the stock negative.
